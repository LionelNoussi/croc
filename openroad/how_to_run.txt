
0. icdesign ihp13 -update all -nogui

Entire flow:

1. Write rtl, testbench and sw
2. Add files to bender in appropriate sections
3. oceda make -B sw
4. oceda make verilator

Simulation works? Lets move over to synthesis:

5. oceda make yosys-flist
6. oceda make yosys                (if you want to keep hierachy on your module, add it to the list in the yosys/scripts/synthesis.tcl script)
7. oceda make sta

After you have a netflist you're satisfied with,
got to floorplan, place, CTS, route in openroad

8. cd openroad          (from croc directory)
9. oceda openroad scripts/S{1-6}_*          (exectue scripts in order. Feel free to look at reports in order as well.)

After sctipt 5, everything is alredy done. S6 is only for analysis really.
Next we have to prepare for DRC&LVS checks.

10. cd klayout          (from croc directory)
11. oceda ./def2gds.sh croc_chip ../openroad/out/croc.def
12. cd calibre/lvs      (from croc directory)
13. ./verilog2spice ../../openroad/out/croc_lvs.v croc_chip.spice

Afterwards fix drc and lvs violations in Calibre, and save final GDS file.
Probably also save the, def and spice files into the openroad out directory, to have a complete folder.
